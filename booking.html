Before your appointment

Two short things that make the session go smoothly.

In person

Arrive 10–15 minutes early

We are at 1281 Hornby Street, #636 — the Bounz Office building. Coming up a few minutes early gives you time to find the entrance, get up to the suite and settle in, so your full session is spent on the actual lesson or repair.

  • Bring the device you need help with, plus its charger
  • Bring any passwords or PINs you might need on the day
  • Buzz #636 if the main door is locked
Online

Join 5–10 minutes early

Find a quiet space with no distractions and open the link a few minutes ahead. That way if the camera, microphone or sound needs sorting out, it happens before the clock starts rather than during your hour.

  • Sit somewhere quiet where you will not be interrupted
  • Plug your computer in, or check the battery is charged
  • Have a pen and paper handy if you like taking notes
