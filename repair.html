What we fix

If it plugs in and misbehaves, it is worth asking about.

Slow computers

Machines that take five minutes to start, freeze on simple tasks, or have slowed to a crawl over the years. Often a drive upgrade or a clean-up rather than a new computer.

Hardware and upgrades

Hard drive to SSD swaps, extra memory, replacement screens, keyboards, batteries and charging ports. We will tell you honestly when a repair is not worth the money.

Viruses and pop-ups

Unwanted software, browser hijacks, endless warnings and scam pop-ups. We clean the machine and show you what to look out for so it does not happen again.

Setup and data transfer

New computer that needs everything moved across, printers that will not connect, email that stopped working, backups that were never set up.

Internet and Wi-Fi

Connections that drop, weak signal in half the home, routers that need configuring, and devices that refuse to join the network.

Not sure what is wrong?
